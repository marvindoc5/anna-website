My Approach

My approach to counselling is collaborative, reflective, and tailored to each individual. Rather than following a fixed method, I aim to create a space where we can explore your experiences with curiosity, openness, and compassion, focusing on what feels most relevant and meaningful to you.

Perspectives I Draw From

Inner Dialogue

The way we speak to ourselves shapes how we feel, relate to others, and make sense of our experiences. Together, we can explore these internal conversations, recognise recurring patterns, and consider ways of responding that feel more aligned with who you are and how you want to live.

Relational & Systemic Perspectives

Our experiences don't happen in isolation. We can consider how your relationships, family, culture, and wider social environment shape the way you see yourself and the world around you.

Acceptance & Commitment Therapy (ACT)

We can find ways to relate differently to difficult thoughts and feelings, clarify what matters to you, and live more in line with your values.

Parts Work

Different sides of ourselves can hold conflicting feelings, needs, or impulses. By getting to know these parts we can develop a deeper understanding of ourselves and our responses.

Hypnosis Techniques

When appropriate, hypnosis can support relaxation, focus, and openness to new perspectives.

I draw from these perspectives flexibly, rather than following a fixed method. Our work is shaped around what feels most relevant and useful to you.

Hypnosis

Hypnosis is one of several tools I may incorporate into our work when it feels appropriate and aligned with your goals. It can offer a way to slow down, focus your attention, and explore thoughts, emotions, and patterns from a different perspective.

Despite common misconceptions, hypnosis does not involve losing control or being unconscious. Instead, it is a natural state of focused attention where you remain aware, engaged, and able to make choices throughout the process.

Whether hypnosis becomes part of our work is always a collaborative decision, guided by what feels most supportive and meaningful for you.
