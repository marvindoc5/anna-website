Biography

My name is Anna Matsi, and I am a Berlin-based psychologist, counsellor, and certified hypnotherapist. I hold a Master's degree in Health and Social Psychology from Maastricht University, alongside professional training in hypnotherapy.

Over the years, I have worked in a variety of settings, supporting neurodivergent individuals, unaccompanied minor refugees, and clients in private practice. Having lived in Cyprus, the Netherlands, and Germany, I have developed an appreciation for the ways culture, relationships, and life experiences shape how we see ourselves, relate to others, and navigate the world around us.

My work is guided by curiosity, compassion, and a deep respect for each person's individual story. I believe that our experiences can only be understood within the context of our unique histories, relationships, and circumstances, and I aim to create space for this complexity in my work with others.
